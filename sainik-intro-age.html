About Sainik School Society

Sainik Schools Society (SSS) is an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India. The Society runs Sainik Schools, which are English medium residential schools affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

Sainik Schools prepare cadets to join the National Defence Academy (NDA), Khadakwasla (Pune), Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala, and other training academies for officers. At present, there are a total of 33 Sainik Schools across the country.
