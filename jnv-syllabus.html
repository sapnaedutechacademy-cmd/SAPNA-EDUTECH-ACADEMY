2. Environmental Studies (EVS) Syllabus

Science & Social

Natural World & Science in Daily Life

  • Flora & Fauna: Plants and animals (land animals and aquatic life).
  • Geography: Rivers, mountains, weather, seasons, transportation, types of houses, and shelters.
  • Natural Cycles: Water cycle and natural disasters.
  • Daily Science: Food preservation, everyday science applications, clean environment, and pollution control (Air, Water, Soil).

Human Body & Health

  • Nutrition: Food items, nutrients, and balanced diet essentials.
  • Hygiene: Personal hygiene and cleanliness habits.
  • Body Systems: Sense organs / super senses, basic digestive system, respiratory system, and circulatory system.

Social Surroundings

  • National Awareness: States, capitals, national symbols, important places in India, and key landmarks.
  • Geography & Culture: Landscapes, India's geographical features, festivals, forests, crops, and clothes/fibres.

Environmental Awareness

  • Conservation: Protection of environment, natural resources, water and soil conservation.
  • Ecology: Relationship between humans, nature, plants, and animals.

4. Arithmetic / Mathematics Test

Numeric Aptitude
A. Number System & Operations
  • Number System and Whole Numbers.
  • Four Fundamental Operations: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division.
  • Factors and Multiples, Prime and Composite Numbers.
  • LCM (Lowest Common Multiple) and HCF (Highest Common Factor).
B. Fractions, Commercial Math & Percentages
  • Fractions & Decimals: Conversion of Fractions and Decimals.
  • Percentage, Profit and Loss, and Simple Interest calculations.
  • Average, Ratio and Proportion, and Unitary Method applications.
C. Measurement, Geometry & Data
  • Measurement: Length, Weight, Capacity, Time, and Money.
  • Geometry: Basic Geometry, Angles, Lines and Shapes, Perimeter, and Area.
  • Data & Application: Data Handling and real-life Word Problems.

5. Language Test

Comprehension Focus

Reading Comprehension

  • Unseen Passage reading.
  • Understanding the Main Idea of the passage.
  • Finding Specific Information from text.

Context & Vocabulary

  • Vocabulary in Context & Meaning of Words.
  • Understanding Sentences & Sequence of Events.
  • Formulating Conclusions/Inference from Passage.
