Building Academic Foundations & Bright Futures

Sapna Edutech Academy is a leading institute for Mathematics, Computer Education, and Competitive Exam Preparation, committed to providing quality education for over 15 years. Our mission is to empower students with strong academic foundations, practical skills, and the confidence to achieve their educational and career goals.

Located in Nayagaon, Mohali, we offer dedicated coaching for students from Class 6 to 12, college and university learners, and aspirants preparing for various competitive examinations. Our experienced faculty focuses on concept-based learning, regular assessments, and individual attention to ensure every student reaches their full potential.
