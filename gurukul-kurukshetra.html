What Makes The Gurukul Unique?

The Gurukul Kurukshetra managed by Arya Pratinidhi Sabha aims to build holistic leaders by uniting traditional Vedic culture with high-level academic standards.

  • Strict residential discipline
  • CBSE academic structure
  • NCC, NSS & sports training

Quality CBSE Academics

Rigorous teaching methodology preparing students for board & competitive exams.

Physical Fitness & Health

Daily sports, morning routines, and outdoor activities to build physical strength.

Moral Character Building

Inculcating Indian cultural values, patriotic spirit, and self-reliance.

Competitive Guidance

Early preparation support for NDA, Defense, NEET, and JEE examinations.
