RMS CET – Class 9 Complete Syllabus

Comprehensive guide covering Mathematics, Science, and English for the Rashtriya Military Schools Entrance Test.

1. Mathematics Syllabus

The mathematics section tests computational skills, logical reasoning, and conceptual clarity across arithmetic, algebra, and geometry.

Unit Topics to Cover
Number System Rational Numbers, Squares & Square Roots, Cubes & Cube Roots, Exponents & Powers
Arithmetic Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion
Applied Mathematics Time & Work, Speed-Time-Distance, Direct & Inverse Proportion, Average
Algebra Algebraic Expressions, Linear Equations, Identities, Factorisation
Geometry Lines & Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Polygons, Circles
Mensuration Perimeter, Area, Surface Area & Volume
Data Handling Data Interpretation, Graphs, Statistics, Probability
Coordinate & Graphs Cartesian Plane, Plotting Points, Simple Graphs
Problem Solving Word Problems, Logical Mathematical Reasoning, Application-based Questions
