🎯 Competition Exam Preparation Stages

A structured overview of the progressive stages required to conquer competitive entrance exams.

Stage Focus Area Objective / Description
Stage 1 Complete NCERT Build a rock-solid foundation by mastering core textbook concepts thoroughly.
Stage 2 Topic-wise MCQ Practice Apply concepts directly with targeted multiple-choice questions per chapter.
Stage 3 Previous / Model RMS Questions Familiarize yourself with actual exam patterns, difficulty levels, and trends.
Stage 4 Full-length Mock Tests Simulate real exam conditions to build stamina, time management, and speed.
Stage 5 Speed & Accuracy Improvement Minimize negative marking and fine-tune calculation performance under pressure.
Final Stage Revision + Mock Tests + Weak Topics Final polish, recurring revision, and targeted rectification of weak conceptual areas.
