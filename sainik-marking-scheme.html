Qualifying Rules & Evaluation Criteria

  • Negative Marking: No negative marking for incorrect answers in either Class 6 or Class 9.
  • General / OBC / Defence category: Minimum 25% marks in each subject and 40% aggregate required.
  • SC / ST category: No minimum subject-wise qualifying marks; admission is based on the merit list and applicable reservation rules.
