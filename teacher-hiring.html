What Makes a Great Fit?

Subject expertise matters, but great teaching also needs patience, communication, consistency, and a genuine interest in student progress.

Strong Subject Knowledge Clear understanding of the subject and its fundamentals.
Clear Communication Ability to explain difficult topics in a simple way.
Student-First Mindset Patience and willingness to understand different learners.
Professional Approach Punctuality, discipline, preparation, and responsibility.
Assessment & Feedback Comfort with tests, practice, feedback, and progress tracking.
Teaching Experience Prior teaching or tutoring experience is valuable.
