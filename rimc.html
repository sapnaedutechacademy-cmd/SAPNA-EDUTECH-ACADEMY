INSTITUTION OVERVIEW

Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC)

Comprehensive Details, Admission Criteria, Age Limits & Exam Patterns

1. Introduction

The Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC) is a prestigious residential educational institution located in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, under the Ministry of Defence[cite: 11]. Established in 1922, it aims to develop academic excellence, discipline, leadership and an interest in the Armed Forces[cite: 11]. RIMC is also a feeder institution for the National Defence Academy (NDA) and other defence-career pathways[cite: 11].

2. Admission

Admission is made to Class VIII[cite: 11]. A candidate must be studying in Class VII or have passed Class VII from a recognised school[cite: 11]. Admissions are conducted twice a year[cite: 11].

Important Age Update

  • For the examination cycles up to June 2026, the age requirement was 11½ to 13 years at the time of admission[cite: 11].
  • From the December 2026 entrance examination onwards, RIMC has announced a revised age range of 12½ to 14 years, applicable for admission to the July 2027 term[cite: 11].

3. Entrance Examination Pattern

Written Examination – 400 Marks[cite: 11]

Subject[cite: 11] Marks[cite: 11]
Mathematics[cite: 11] 200[cite: 11]
English[cite: 11] 125[cite: 11]
General Knowledge[cite: 11] 75[cite: 11]
Total[cite: 11] 400[cite: 11]

Viva-Voce – 50 Marks[cite: 11]

Candidates qualifying in the written examination are called for a Viva-Voce test of 50 marks[cite: 11]. It assesses areas such as intelligence, personality and confidence[cite: 11]. The minimum qualifying marks are 50%[cite: 11].

Medical Examination[cite: 11]

Candidates who qualify the interview undergo a medical examination at a designated Military Hospital[cite: 11]. Medical fitness is essential for final selection[cite: 11].

4. Selection Process

Written Examination Viva-Voce Medical Examination Final Merit/Selection[cite: 11]

Selection is highly competitive and is based on merit, subject to the prescribed eligibility and medical standards[cite: 11].
