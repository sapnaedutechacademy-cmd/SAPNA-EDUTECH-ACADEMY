1. RIMC Entrance Examination Pattern

The entrance examination consists of a written examination (400 Marks), followed by a Viva-Voce test (50 Marks) for qualifying candidates and a medical examination.

Subject Total Marks Qualifying Cut-off
Mathematics 200 Marks 50%
English 125 Marks 50%
General Knowledge 75 Marks 50%
Written Exam Total 400 Marks 50% Aggregate
Viva-Voce / Interview 50 Marks 50%

7. Life at RIMC

RIMC provides a residential public-school education combined with physical training, sports, leadership development, discipline and outdoor activities. The academic programme is designed for holistic development and also provides preparation relevant to future NDA/defence aspirations.

8. Key Points for Parents & Students

Entry Class VIII
Current revised age from Dec 2026 exam cycle 12½–14 years
Written Exam 400 Marks
Viva 50 Marks
Subjects English, Mathematics, General Knowledge
Final Stages Written + Viva + Medical
Institution Ministry of Defence
Location Dehradun, Uttarakhand
Nature Residential, highly competitive, defence-oriented education
