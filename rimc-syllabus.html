EXAMINATION & ADMISSION GUIDE

Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC) Syllabus

Comprehensive Topic-Wise Breakdown, Exam Pattern, Marks Scheme & Life at RIMC

1. RIMC Entrance Examination Pattern

The entrance examination consists of a written examination (400 Marks), followed by a Viva-Voce test (50 Marks) for qualifying candidates and a medical examination.

Subject Total Marks Qualifying Cut-off
Mathematics 200 Marks 50%
English 125 Marks 50%
General Knowledge 75 Marks 50%
Written Exam Total 400 Marks 50% Aggregate
Viva-Voce / Interview 50 Marks 50%

4. General Knowledge – 75 Marks

75 Marks
A. Current Affairs & Defence Awareness
  • National/International events, schemes, appointments, awards, and sports
  • Defence Focus: Armed Forces structure (Army, Navy, Air Force), Commands, Ranks, Insignia, Gallantry Awards, and NDA/IMA/RIMC institutions
B. Indian History & Geography
  • History: Ancient (Indus Valley, Vedic, Mauryan, Gupta), Medieval (Sultanate, Mughals, Marathas), Modern (British Rule, 1857 Revolt, Freedom Movement, Gandhi Era)
  • Geography: States, Capitals, Rivers, Mountains, Climate, Monsoons, Agriculture, Soils, Minerals, and Ports
C. General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Physics: Force, Motion, Energy, Light, Sound, Heat, Electricity, Magnetism
  • Chemistry: States of Matter, Elements, Compounds, Acids/Bases, Physical/Chemical changes
  • Biology: Human Body Organs, Nutrition, Respiration, Plants, Microorganisms, Ecosystems
D. Indian Polity, Sports & Miscellaneous GK
  • Polity: Constitution, Fundamental Rights/Duties, President, PM, Parliament, Judiciary, Panchayati Raj
  • Sports: Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth, Cricket, Football, Awards
  • Static GK: Countries/Capitals, Currencies, Books & Authors, Inventions, Firsts in India/World

6. Viva-Voce / Interview Preparation

50 Marks

Assesses intelligence, personality, confidence, and leadership potential.

Personal & School Profile

Self-introduction, reason for joining RIMC & Armed Forces, hobbies, strengths, and family background.

Academic & GK Questions

Basic Math, General Science concepts, English vocabulary, current affairs, and national symbols.

Defence Awareness

President, Prime Minister, Chiefs of Armed Forces, and major military institutions.

Personality & Conduct

Clear speech, listening skills, discipline, confidence, and positive body language.
