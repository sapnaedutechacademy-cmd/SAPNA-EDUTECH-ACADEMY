EXAMINATION & ADMISSION GUIDE

Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC) Syllabus

Comprehensive Topic-Wise Breakdown, Exam Pattern, Marks Scheme & Life at RIMC

1. RIMC Entrance Examination Pattern

The entrance examination consists of a written examination (400 Marks), followed by a Viva-Voce test (50 Marks) for qualifying candidates and a medical examination.

Subject Total Marks Qualifying Cut-off
Mathematics 200 Marks 50%
English 125 Marks 50%
General Knowledge 75 Marks 50%
Written Exam Total 400 Marks 50% Aggregate
Viva-Voce / Interview 50 Marks 50%

2. Mathematics – 200 Marks

200 Marks
A. Number System & Basic Arithmetic
  • Natural numbers, whole numbers, and integers
  • Positive/negative numbers, place value, and face value
  • Number comparison, number line, even/odd, prime & composite numbers
  • Factors, multiples, HCF, LCM, divisibility rules, and prime factorisation
  • BODMAS / Order of operations
B. Fractions, Decimals & Percentages
  • Proper, improper, and mixed fractions; equivalent fractions & simplification
  • Decimal operations, conversions, and comparison
  • Conversion between fractions, decimals, and percentages
  • Finding percentages, percentage change, and word problems
C. Commercial Math (Ratio, Profit/Loss, Interest)
  • Ratio, equivalent ratios, simplest form, proportion & unitary method
  • Cost price, selling price, marked price, profit/loss percentage & discount
  • Simple Interest calculations
  • Average: Simple average, missing values, and group averages
D. Algebra, Geometry & Mensuration
  • Algebraic expressions, terms, coefficients, linear equations & identities
  • Angles, lines, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and symmetry
  • Perimeter, area of circles/polygons, basic volume, and unit conversion
  • Speed, Distance, Time, and elementary trains/relative movement
E. Data Handling & Measurement
  • Tables, pictographs, bar graphs, and line graphs interpretation
  • Basic probability concepts
  • Measurement unit conversions (Length, Mass, Capacity, Time, Money)

3. English – 125 Marks

125 Marks

Grammar & Structure

  • Parts of Speech: Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Articles
  • Tenses: Simple, Continuous, Perfect forms & correct usage
  • Subject-Verb Agreement: Rules and common errors
  • Sentence Structure: Subject & predicate, sentence types

Vocabulary & Comprehension

  • Synonyms, Antonyms, and One-word substitutions
  • Homophones, Homonyms, Prefixes, Suffixes & Spellings
  • Idioms, phrases, and commonly confused words
  • Unseen Prose & Poetry Comprehension passages

Sentence Skills

  • Fill in the blanks & sentence completion
  • Rearrangement of jumbled sentences
  • Error identification & sentence improvement

Writing & Literary Awareness

  • Paragraph writing & short descriptive passages
  • Formal & informal letter / application formats
  • Story-based comprehension

4. General Knowledge – 75 Marks

75 Marks
A. Current Affairs & Defence Awareness
  • National/International events, schemes, appointments, awards, and sports
  • Defence Focus: Armed Forces structure (Army, Navy, Air Force), Commands, Ranks, Insignia, Gallantry Awards, and NDA/IMA/RIMC institutions
B. Indian History & Geography
  • History: Ancient (Indus Valley, Vedic, Mauryan, Gupta), Medieval (Sultanate, Mughals, Marathas), Modern (British Rule, 1857 Revolt, Freedom Movement, Gandhi Era)
  • Geography: States, Capitals, Rivers, Mountains, Climate, Monsoons, Agriculture, Soils, Minerals, and Ports
C. General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Physics: Force, Motion, Energy, Light, Sound, Heat, Electricity, Magnetism
  • Chemistry: States of Matter, Elements, Compounds, Acids/Bases, Physical/Chemical changes
  • Biology: Human Body Organs, Nutrition, Respiration, Plants, Microorganisms, Ecosystems
D. Indian Polity, Sports & Miscellaneous GK
  • Polity: Constitution, Fundamental Rights/Duties, President, PM, Parliament, Judiciary, Panchayati Raj
  • Sports: Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth, Cricket, Football, Awards
  • Static GK: Countries/Capitals, Currencies, Books & Authors, Inventions, Firsts in India/World

5. Reasoning & Mental Ability

Evaluated within the examination framework to test analytical speed and logical aptitude.

Verbal Reasoning

Analogy (Number, Letter, Word) Classification / Odd One Out Coding-Decoding Blood Relations Direction Sense

Series & Puzzles

Number & Alphabet Series Ranking & Ordering Venn Diagrams Seating & Age Puzzles

Non-Verbal Reasoning

Mirror & Water Images Paper Folding / Cutting Embedded Figures Counting Figures

6. Viva-Voce / Interview Preparation

50 Marks

Assesses intelligence, personality, confidence, and leadership potential.

Personal & School Profile

Self-introduction, reason for joining RIMC & Armed Forces, hobbies, strengths, and family background.

Academic & GK Questions

Basic Math, General Science concepts, English vocabulary, current affairs, and national symbols.

Defence Awareness

President, Prime Minister, Chiefs of Armed Forces, and major military institutions.

Personality & Conduct

Clear speech, listening skills, discipline, confidence, and positive body language.
